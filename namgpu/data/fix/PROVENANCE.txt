NOAA/NCEP fix files read by the RRTMG radiation init and the in-run CO2 update (the NMMB run
directory's fix files; exnam_fcst.sh links them by these names). Unmodified copies of the NOAA
global fix set, fix/am/20220805, from https://noaa-nws-global-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/fix/am/20220805/
(co2historicaldata_YYYY.txt from co2dat_4a/global_co2historicaldata_YYYY.txt; 2025 and 2026 from
the *_proj files). US Government data. Fetched 2026-11-04 (2004-2014 added 2026-11-06 for archived-GFS hindcasts); tools/fetch_fix.sh fetches them again.
The Noah tables in noah/ are described in noah/PROVENANCE.txt.
